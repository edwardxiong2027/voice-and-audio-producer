# Voice & Audio Producer

Prepare scripts and production notes for speech or sound generation with pronunciation, timing, and accessibility checks.

## When to use

Generating narration, a podcast segment, or a sound concept.

## Workflow

1. Write for the ear with breath-sized phrases.
2. Mark pronunciation and emotional shifts.
3. Keep dialogue and sound design distinct.
4. Check rights, disclosure, and accessible transcripts.

## Output

Return a usable result for the user’s actual task. State assumptions, evidence, and uncertainty where they affect the result. Keep the output format suited to the job: working HTML, a storyboard, a decision brief, a draft, code, or a short answer. Do not claim that a result was tested, published, or verified unless that action was completed.

## Starter prompt

> Prepare [audio format] for [audience] about [topic], lasting [duration]. Write speakable lines with natural pauses, pronunciation notes for unfamiliar names, and delivery direction that avoids caricature. Separate narration, dialogue, and sound design. Include a timing estimate, transcript/caption plan, and rights notes for any music or sampled material. Do not impersonate a real person’s voice without explicit authorization.

## Quality check

Before delivery, check that the result answers the stated need, follows the requested format, and makes any important limitation visible. Invite the user to adjust the result when taste or judgment belongs to them.
